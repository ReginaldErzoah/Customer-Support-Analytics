# Business Questions

## Overview

The Customer Support Analytics project was developed to analyze customer support operations and provide insights into service efficiency, customer experience, and product-related issues.

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions that help support teams improve operational performance and customer satisfaction.

---

# Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this analytics solution are:

* Understand overall customer support workload
* Measure support team responsiveness and efficiency
* Identify factors affecting customer satisfaction
* Detect products generating high support demand
* Understand customer behaviour patterns
* Support data-driven decisions for improving support operations

---

# Support Operations Analysis

## 1. What is the overall volume of customer support activity?

### Business Question

How many customer support requests are being received, and what does the overall support workload look like?

### Metrics Used

* Total number of tickets
* Number of unique customers
* Ticket distribution by type
* Ticket distribution by priority
* Ticket distribution by channel

### Business Value

Helps management understand:

* Support demand levels
* Resource requirements
* Workload distribution across support channels

---

# Ticket Type Analysis

## 2. What are the most common reasons customers contact support?

### Business Question

Which support issues generate the highest ticket volume?

### Metrics Used

* Ticket count by ticket type
* Percentage contribution by ticket category

Examples:

* Refund requests
* Technical issues
* Cancellation requests
* Billing inquiries
* Product inquiries

### Business Value

Helps identify:

* Common customer pain points
* Areas requiring process improvement
* Opportunities for automation or self-service solutions

# Support Performance Analysis

### Business Question

### Business Value

Supports:

### Business Question

# Summary

The Customer Support Analytics project converts raw support interactions into actionable business insights.

By combining data transformation, quality validation, dimensional modeling, and visualization, the solution provides stakeholders with a reliable view of customer support performance and improvement opportunities.
